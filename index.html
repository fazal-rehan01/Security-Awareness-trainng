  2. 2 Is my home, street or location showing?

03 · Seniors

Urgent can wait. Check first.

Big-text rules for WhatsApp, phone calls, OTPs and sudden money requests.

A message you might get

Papa, my phone broke. Send ₹25,000 to this account now. Don't call — I am in a meeting.

URGENT NEW NUMBER

Two red flags are already visible: urgency and a new number.

01

Call the saved number

Use the number already saved in your phone. Do not use the number sent in the message.

02

Slow down when it is urgent

Scammers use fear and deadlines so you act fast. Take five minutes and tell someone you trust.

03

Never share an OTP or PIN

No bank, police officer or family member needs your OTP. Whoever has it can take your money.

Your OTP is a key. Never hand it to anyone.

Quick check

What would you do?

The message says: “Your bank account will be blocked today. Share the OTP on your phone immediately.”

If you already shared an OTP, call your bank immediately and report it on 1930.

04 · Women & girls

Save proof. Lock down. Report.

A cybersecurity toolkit for online harassment, stalking and image abuse. Your evidence matters, and your privacy settings are your first line of defence.

Step one

Save the evidence before you block.

Do not delete the messages yet. Preserve a clear, unedited record first.

  1. 1

    Capture the full screen

    Show the username, message, date, time and app or URL. Do not crop the screenshot.

  2. 2

    Keep the original safe

    Save the unedited screenshot or chat export somewhere private. Never edit the original.

  3. 3

    Write a short log

    Record what happened, when, the account name and the link. Keep it factual.

  4. 4

    Record every handover

    Note each time you share a copy with a platform, the police or a lawyer. That is your chain of custody.

Chain of custody, in plain words

It is a simple record of where the evidence came from and who handled it. Keep the first copy unchanged.

Step two

Lock down your accounts.

Small privacy changes make it harder for an abuser to find you, tag you or track your location.

Account lockdown

IG Instagram

Make the account private, then limit tags and mentions to people you follow.

Official Instagram guide
FB Facebook

Set posts to Friends. Use Lock profile where available, plus tag and timeline review.

Official Facebook guide
Location tagging

Turn off location for social apps in phone settings. Remove location tags from new and old posts.
